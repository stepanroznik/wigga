Wigga is based on Wiggly by MojArch and contributors.
https://github.com/Arch-Lover/wiggly

Wiggly is based on Wiggle by mechtifs and contributors.
https://github.com/mechtifs/wiggle

Wiggle derives its cursor scaling effect from Jiggle by Jeff Channell.
https://github.com/jeffchannell/jiggle

The live cursor texture rendering follows GNOME Shell's Magnifier.
https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-shell/-/blob/46.0/js/ui/magnifier.js
